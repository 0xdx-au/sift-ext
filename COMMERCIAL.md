This code is **publicly viewable**, not automatically open-source.

ADMINE retains the option to sell commercial licences, subscriptions,
enterprise features, distribution rights or support packages later.
A public repository does not grant third parties a right to sell or
repackage ADMINE-authored code.

A future change to MIT, Apache-2.0, GPL or another licence must be a
deliberate decision: rights already granted to recipients generally
cannot be withdrawn retroactively.

Review external contributors' rights before merging code; consider a
contributor agreement if dual licensing is planned.

or sharing obligations independently of ADMINE's own licence. Check
the current licence for each upstream list before commercial release.

No sales system or commercial entitlement is implemented yet.
